# Pantry Pal — product concept

## Vision
Families waste food and money because nobody knows what is in the kitchen or what to buy. Pantry Pal gives a household one shared place for the grocery list and the pantry, so shopping is faster and less food is thrown away.

## Who it is for
Households of 2–6 people who share grocery shopping (parents, flatmates). Most use the app on a phone, often in a store with patchy signal.

## Core features (version 1)
1. **Shared grocery list.** Anyone in the household adds, ticks off and removes items; everyone sees changes quickly.
2. **Pantry inventory.** Record what is at home with quantity and expiry date. Items nearing expiry are highlighted.
3. **Expiry reminders.** A morning notification: "3 things expire in the next 2 days".
4. **Quick add by barcode.** Point the phone camera at a product to add it.
5. **"What can I cook?"** Suggest meals from what is in the pantry, prioritising food that expires soon.
6. **Works in the store.** The list must still be usable and tickable when the connection drops, and sync afterwards.

## Plans and money
Free for small households; a **Premium** plan unlocks unlimited items and meal suggestions. Price to be decided.

## Principles
No ads. We never sell household data. A household's data is visible only to its members.

## Success looks like
A household that adds its first 20 pantry items in the first week and still uses the list a month later.
